Field Notes is a fictional series shipped with the template. This is part two. The series page collects every post whose frontmatter `series` matches the series slug.

### How parts are ordered

`partOrder` is a number. Lower numbers come first. Dates stay on the post itself so the writings index can still sort by time.

### Publishing elsewhere

If a piece lives on another site, set `externalUrl` to an `https://example.com/...` address. The writings index will link out, and the local article route will redirect there.

You can delete this file when your own series is ready.
